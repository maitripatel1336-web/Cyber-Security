ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understanding Cryptography

SecureText is a browser-based cybersecurity project developed to demonstrate the fundamental concepts of text encryption and decryption.

It provides a simple interface for converting readable messages into encrypted text and recovering them using the correct password.

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript AES
🎯

Project Objective

To develop a simple web-based application that demonstrates how encryption and decryption can help protect the confidentiality of text messages.

STAY SECURITY AWARE

Protect Your Passwords

Follow these basic practices when working with encrypted messages.

01

Use Strong Passwords

Choose a long, unique password that is difficult for others to guess.

02

Keep Your Password Private

Anyone who obtains your password and encrypted message may be able to read it.

03

Keep a Secure Backup

Store important encrypted files safely and remember your password.

04

Verify Before Sharing

Do not share confidential messages or passwords with untrusted people.
